This is a sample post — here to show how the blog works rather than to say anything in particular. Replace it with your first real post whenever you're ready, or just delete it.

Each post is its own HTML file living in blog/posts/, written directly rather than through any templating system. Open blog/posts/_post-template.html, duplicate it under a new name, and start writing. Paragraphs are plain <p> tags — nothing fancier required.

This is what a two-photo layout looks like — the template has the real, uncommented markup ready to use.

Photos drop into blog/images/posts/ and get referenced with an <img> tag — either on their own with a caption underneath, or side by side like the pair above. Both patterns are in the template, commented out, ready to uncomment and point at your own files.

Once a post is written, it won't appear on the Blog page automatically — add one short entry to js/data/blog-posts.js with its title, date, a one-line excerpt, and a link to the file, and it'll show up there as a card. Full details are in the project's README.md.
